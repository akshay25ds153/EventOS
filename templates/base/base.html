Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about the EventOS

🚀 Getting Started

Navigate to the Login page and enter your username and password. If you don't have an account, click Register to create one. After successful login you will be redirected to the Dashboard.

On the Login page, click Forgot Password. Enter your registered email address and follow the instructions sent to your inbox to reset your password.

Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select Profile. From there you can upload a profile photo, update your name, email, phone number, and bio. Click Save Changes when done.

📅 Events

Go to Events → Add Event from the sidebar. Fill in the event name, category, date, venue, and description, then click Save. The event will appear in the Events list immediately.

Open the event from the Events list and click the Edit (pencil) icon. Make your changes and click Save. To delete, click the Delete (trash) icon and confirm the deletion in the popup.

The Dashboard shows upcoming events sorted by date. If the event date is in the past, it won't appear in the upcoming section. Check the Events list for a full view of all events.

👥 Members

Go to Members → Add Member. Enter the member's name, email, phone, and event association, then save. Members can also be registered directly from an event's detail page.

Use the Search bar at the top of the Members list page. You can filter by name, email, or event. The global navbar search (top of screen) also finds members instantly.

📍 Venues

Navigate to Venues → Add Venue. Provide the venue name, address, capacity, and any additional notes. Once saved, the venue will be available to select when creating or editing events.

You must create a venue first before it appears in the event form dropdown. Go to Venues → Add Venue, save it, then return to the event form to select it.

💰 Budget

Go to Budgets from the sidebar. Click Add Budget and link it to an event. You can add individual expense items with categories, amounts, and descriptions. The system automatically calculates totals and shows you overspend alerts.

A negative balance means your total expenses exceed the allocated budget. Review your expense items in the Budget detail page and either increase the allocated amount or reduce expenses.

🏪 Vendors & Contracts

Go to Vendors → Add Vendor. Fill in the vendor's name, service type, and contact details. Once saved, you can link them to events via Contracts and track service agreements.

Go to Contracts → Add Contract. Select the vendor, associated event, start/end dates, and agreed cost. You can attach contract documents and set payment status. Contracts are tracked separately from budget items.

🎫 Tickets & QR Check-in

Each registered member receives a unique QR code ticket. At the event entrance, open QR Scanner from the sidebar. Allow camera access and point the webcam at the attendee's QR code. The system will instantly validate and mark them as checked-in.

Make sure you have allowed camera permissions in your browser. The site must be served over HTTPS (or localhost) for camera access to work. Check your browser's address bar for a camera icon and click it to grant access.

This means the QR code is either not registered in the system, belongs to a different event, or has already been used for check-in (duplicate scan). Verify the ticket in the Members list and regenerate if necessary.

📦 Resources

Navigate to Resources → Add Resource. You can log equipment (projectors, microphones, tables, etc.), specify quantity, and assign them to events. The resource list helps you track availability and avoid double-booking.

💬 Chat

Open the Chat section from the sidebar. You'll see a list of team members on the left. Click on a member's name to open the conversation and type your message in the input box at the bottom.

Ensure you have a stable internet connection. If the issue persists, try refreshing the page. The chat system requires WebSocket support — make sure your browser is up to date (Chrome, Firefox, Edge recommended).

⚙️ General & UI

Click the Palette icon (🎨) in the top navbar to open the Personalize drawer. Toggle the Dark Mode switch to enable it. Your preference is saved in the browser and persists across sessions.
